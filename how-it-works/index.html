How we source, quote and deliver.

Written for buyers who need to know exactly what they are dealing with before they commit an order — what happens at each stage, what you receive in writing, and where the risks sit.

01

Send your list

Type items into the quote form, paste an existing list, upload an LPO or BQ, or send a photo of a handwritten note on WhatsApp.

2 minutes

02

We source and price it

We check availability with our suppliers, confirm specifications with you where needed, and return one itemised quotation.

Within 24 hours

03

You approve

Approve by email, WhatsApp or LPO. Payment terms are agreed in writing before we dispatch — no surprises on delivery day.

Written terms

04

We deliver, complete

Goods arrive on the agreed date with a delivery note and invoice. Short-supplied items are flagged before dispatch, not after.

Questions buyers ask first

Before you send a list.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
Yes — upcountry delivery is quoted per location and load, shown as a separate line on your quotation.
Is there a minimum order?
No. Small orders and single items are quoted the same way as bulk orders.
Can you work with an LPO?
Yes. Send the LPO with your request and we’ll respond with matching documentation.
What if an item isn’t on your list?
Send it anyway. Special orders are routine — we source against your specification.
